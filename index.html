Local talent: Cancer Bats

(11/23/2026)

Local to Toronto, Cancer Bats is a solid Canadian hardcore punk band, playing at local events like PRFM in london, ON they have eight studio albums, the most recent as of writing this section being “Give me dirt” released Aug, 7, 2026. Personally i love this band and recommend the “Hail destroyer” album.
